About Me

Engineering reliable systems behind the scenes

I'm a systems-minded engineer who builds the automation and integration layer that keeps modern SaaS platforms running. My work spans building and deploying AI-powered agents, engineering workflow automation, and configuring enterprise applications — with a strong foundation in SQL reporting, API integrations (REST & SOAP), and cross-platform troubleshooting.

Over the past 5+ years, my career has grown from hands-on web development into implementation engineering, and now into designing AI-driven automation solutions — developing and optimizing prompts, implementing LLMs and AI agents, and integrating automation workflows with CRM, ERP, and communication systems for enterprise clients.

I care about translating ambiguous business requirements into scalable, well-documented technical solutions, and about collaborating closely with Technical Solutions Managers, developers, architects, QA, and support teams to ship dependable systems — not just quick fixes.
